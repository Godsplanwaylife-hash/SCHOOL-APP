Everything on Our Crest Tells Our Story

Four ideas guide every day at our school.

01

Faith

Our motto, “In God We Trust”, is the foundation we build on. Children learn to value honesty, kindness and respect.

02

Knowledge

The book on our crest stands for learning from A to Z, with strong teaching in reading, writing and numbers.

03

Discovery

The microscope reminds us to stay curious, ask questions and learn how the world works.

04

Excellence

The eagle at the top invites every child to aim high and give their best.

Questions Parents Ask

How do I enrol my child?

Please visit the school or contact the school office using the details below. The office will guide you through the next steps.

Which classes does the school have?

We have Nursery 1 to Nursery 4 and Primary 1 to Primary 4.

How do I get a portal login?

Login details are created by the school office. When your account is added, a welcome email is sent to the address you gave the school.

What colours is the school uniform?

Our uniform is red and navy blue, the same colours you see on this website.
